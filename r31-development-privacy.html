Information used by this build

Online play requires an internet connection and Game Center sign-in. We verify an Apple-signed, app-scoped player identifier and associate it with an internal game account. We do not receive your Apple password or payment-card information.

Device and iCloud storage

The device holds account-bound save and retry journals, presentation preferences and an age-range choice. We ask for an age range, not a birth date. When iCloud is available, a signed game snapshot is backed up to your private CloudKit database. A client-written backup cannot grant inventory or replace the server's authoritative progress. Apple controls Game Center and iCloud accounts.

Advertising and rewarded videos

The current integration uses Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) supplies applicable consent and privacy-choice forms. AdMob ad serving is not enabled by the UMP integration. Automatic Meta advertising/event reporting and the legacy Firebase paths are disabled in this reviewed test build.

Advertising providers may process device and advertising identifiers allowed by platform settings, IP addresses, device/app information, ad requests, impressions, interactions and performance information. These support advertising, measurement, frequency limits, security and fraud prevention. A request for contextual ads does not mean that no information is processed.

The app supplies LevelPlay with a pseudonymous ad-user identifier obtained from the game authority. It does not supply a raw Game Center identifier or game authentication token. This identifier is pseudonymous, not anonymous. For rewarded videos, LevelPlay sends the authority a signed completion event with that identifier, an event ID, reward amount and verification information. One valid event grants one Continue once. Closing an ad or clicking an advertiser's install button is not a reward condition.

When ads are available, a banner can appear at the bottom of screens. An interstitial opportunity occurs after every two accepted campaign wins. Game-over rewarded videos are optional, and Restart remains free. Unavailable ads or pending reward verification do not remove free Restart.

Age, consent and privacy choices

Witchdoku is intended for children and adults. In this registered-device development ad test, only the adult range can initialize advertising. Under-13, teen and unknown-age choices do not start the ad SDK. The planned child path uses child-directed, restricted-consent and do-not-sell signals, but it remains disabled pending provider and device review. Online gameplay still uses the account and game information described above. Parents can contact us about a child's information and review Apple's Game Center and iCloud controls.

On the eligible adult path, the app refreshes UMP consent information and presents any required form before requesting ads. A failed consent request does not silently authorize advertising. Apple's tracking permission is separate; declining it does not prevent playing, though this registered-IDFA ad test may remain unavailable.

Use Settings → Ad privacy to revisit the age range, retry consent or open required provider privacy options. Changes pause new ad requests and presentation. If the ad SDK has already started, the app asks you to close and reopen Witchdoku to apply the new setup fully. You can also change Apple's tracking permission in iOS Settings. The integration sends the ad provider a do-not-sell signal.

Deletion and purchase restoration

Settings → Delete My Data requires explicit confirmation. The flow revokes game sessions, removes the live profile, progress, ranking eligibility, settings, feedback and inventory, clears the device's account data, and removes the signed iCloud payload. A small deletion marker remains to prevent an older backup from restoring erased progress. Interrupted requests remain pending and can be retried; the app does not report success before server and cloud acknowledgement. Disconnected devices must reconnect, and their local copies cannot be remotely erased immediately.

Deletion does not delete your Apple account or refund or cancel Apple purchases. Starting again requires an explicit choice. Eligible permanent cosmetics require fresh Apple verification to restore; spent or deleted consumables are not regranted. Limited pseudonymous purchase, reward-event and account-binding records remain to prevent duplicate grants and to support eligible restoration.

Retention and development recovery copies

Live game information is retained while the account is active, until deletion or the end of this development service. Limited transaction, anti-replay and deletion records remain for security, purchase integrity and eligible restoration. A deletion acknowledgement is usable for retries for 30 days; short-lived proof hashes have six-minute validity. Expired authentication records are pruned when authentication state next changes, so inactive records can remain longer.

Separate development recovery copies are kept privately by the operator. In-app deletion does not automatically erase these copies, and this development environment has no fixed automatic backup-cleanup schedule. Contact us to request manual review and deletion of copies we hold. A verified retention and deletion-propagation schedule is required before public rollout. This notice does not promise completed erasure of recovery copies.
