R31 development service information

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This page describes the current iOS R31 development test. The existing Witchdoku Terms of Service remain available separately.

Play and saves

Online play requires an internet connection and Game Center sign-in. The game authority validates progress, scores, daily rewards, saves and inventory. Original Witch and Vampire and free Restart remain available without a purchase.

Purchases and ads

Optional purchases use Apple StoreKit and Apple's displayed checkout price. Cancelled or pending payments grant nothing until verification and durable delivery. Restore Purchases recovers eligible permanent cosmetics; spent or deleted consumables are not restored as new stock. Apple's process handles refunds, and deleting game data does not refund or cancel a purchase. Mandatory consumer rights remain applicable.

Current ad testing is limited to a registered adult test device. Subject to availability and applicable privacy choices, banners may appear at the bottom, interstitial opportunities occur after every two accepted campaign wins, and a game-over rewarded video is optional. One valid server-confirmed rewarded event grants one Continue. Closing an ad alone grants nothing. Free Restart remains available when ads fail or verification is pending.

Privacy and deletion

Use Settings → Ad privacy for available age and consent choices. Delete My Data requires explicit confirmation and removes live game progress and inventory through the server/cloud acknowledgement flow. Pending deletion is not completed deletion. Starting a new game requires an explicit choice, with fresh Apple verification for eligible permanent purchases. Limited anti-replay and transaction records and separate development recovery copies are retained as described in the R31 development privacy notice. Current backup cleanup is manual.
