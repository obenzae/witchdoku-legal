Witchdoku iOS service information

Updated October 24, 2026

Witchdoku online play requires an internet connection and Game Center sign-in. Keep using the same Game Center account to access your validated progress. App Store, TestFlight and earlier development services keep separate progress and purchase environments.

Campaign progress, tools, character ownership and rankings come from the online game authority. A failed load does not confirm that a new account should be created. If sign-in or loading fails, reconnect and retry; avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step. If Apple does not show its sign-in screen, open the Settings app, choose Game Center, sign in and return to Witchdoku.

Apple manages purchase payments and refund requests. Use Restore for eligible character entitlements; already-spent consumables are not replenished. Delayed or pending transactions require Apple approval and verified delivery. A purchase prompt alone does not grant an item.

Ads can be eligible for all age ranges under the applicable privacy restrictions. Availability varies; optional rewarded videos do not replace free Restart. Refusing tracking keeps optional measurement off while restricted ad delivery can remain available where permitted. Play reminders are optional and can be disabled in Settings.

For technical or privacy support, contact benlalououssama@gmail.com. Provide the app version, device model, a description and approximate time of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment details, authentication tokens or advertising identifiers.
