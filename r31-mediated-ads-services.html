Witchdoku internal beta

Updated: 15 October 2026

For internal TestFlight builds of Witchdoku 1.0 linking here. This describes the beta service; it does not replace or accept the existing Terms of Service or announce a public release.

Honesty Games operates Witchdoku. Online gameplay requires internet access and Game Center sign-in. Server verification governs progress, scores, saves and inventory. The original Witch and Vampire and free Restart remain available without a purchase.

Eligible adults may receive banner ads supplied through Unity LevelPlay by ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, Google AdMob or Meta Audience Network and an interstitial opportunity after every two accepted campaign wins. Ads depend on availability, privacy choices and provider eligibility. Ad failure or no fill does not create a later catch-up interstitial. Under-13, teen and unknown-age advertising remain disabled.

Separate Meta App Events and automatic purchase-event reporting remain disabled. Ad-network preparation does not establish live fill or revenue.

Game-over rewarded video is optional. One valid provider-signed event, accepted by the server, grants one Continue once. Closing an ad or clicking its install button is not a reward condition. Delayed or failed verification grants no unconfirmed inventory and keeps free Restart available.

Apple tracking permission and applicable UMP consent are separate choices. Declining tracking does not block gameplay. Use Settings → Ad privacy to revisit the age range or provider privacy options. If advertising has already started, close and reopen the app to apply a changed privacy plan.

Apple TestFlight purchases use the sandbox and do not charge real money. Eligible permanent cosmetics require fresh Apple verification to restore; used or deleted consumables are not restored as new stock. No purchase is required for ad consent or Restart.

Settings → Delete My Data requires explicit confirmation; pending deletion is not completion. Full signed device/cloud deletion and backup restoration behavior remain under verification. Contact us for assistance. Data retention and the separate development and TestFlight environments are described in the internal beta privacy notice.
