Witchdoku iOS privacy notice

Updated October 24, 2026

This notice describes the Witchdoku iOS version that links here. Witchdoku uses an online game service and Game Center identity to save progress, deliver purchases and maintain rankings. Advertising, tracking permission and optional reminders are separate choices. Refusing tracking or reminders does not prevent playing.

Game accounts and progress

Online play requires an internet connection and Game Center sign-in. Apple supplies a signed, app-scoped player identifier. Our service verifies it and associates it with an internal game account. We do not receive your Apple password or payment-card information. App Store, TestFlight and earlier development environments use separate service accounts and inventory.

The game service stores campaign and tutorial progress, puzzle sessions, scores, tool balances, character ownership and selection, achievements or daily rewards, streak dates, settings, and account or transaction records needed to operate these features. Rankings use accepted game results. Connection-derived country information may accompany ranking entries; it is not a statement of nationality.

Device and iCloud storage

Your device stores account-bound pending-operation and completion journals, presentation preferences and an age-range choice. We request an age range rather than your birth date. Authentication credentials use the device's protected storage. When available, the game can save a signed snapshot to your private CloudKit database. The online game service remains authoritative; an iCloud copy cannot grant purchases or replace validated progress. Apple controls Game Center and iCloud account settings.

Purchases and purchase integrity

Apple processes in-app purchases and provides product prices and transaction information. Our service verifies matching app, product, account and environment information before delivering an item. Purchase, revocation and reward records prevent duplicate grants and support eligible restoration. Consumables already spent are not replenished by Restore. Apple manages billing, cancellations and refund decisions.

Advertising and data used for delivery

Witchdoku uses Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, Google AdMob and, on eligible adult paths, Meta Audience Network. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides applicable regional consent and privacy-choice forms. Ad availability depends on the privacy choices, provider eligibility and available inventory. Rewarded videos are optional and disclose the offered reward; restarting a puzzle remains available without watching one.

Advertising and consent providers may process IP address and approximate location inferred from a connection, app and device information, identifiers permitted by platform settings, consent choices, ad requests, views, interactions, performance and diagnostic information. These support delivery, security, fraud prevention and permitted reporting. Restricted or contextual ads still involve data processing; they are not described as anonymous.

Age ranges and restricted advertising

All age-range choices can be eligible for advertising. Under-13, teen and unknown-age choices use a child-directed setup with restricted consent, do-not-sell signals, Google child-age treatment and a general-audience ad-content rating. Their mediation groups use ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google and exclude Meta Audience Network. These choices do not request tracking permission, access the advertising identifier through app code, or start Meta App Events or Google Analytics. They can still use the online account and game information described above. Parents may contact us about a child's information and use Apple's account and privacy controls.

Consent and Apple's tracking permission

For adults, the app refreshes Google's consent information and completes any required regional form before requesting Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. The form is not forced on every launch when a valid choice is already stored. Google consent messaging is configured for visitors worldwide; the available choices and legal requirements can vary by region.

If the regional choice refuses required tracking purposes, the app does not request ATT. If ATT is denied or restricted, the app does not enable cross-company tracking or its optional Meta and Google measurement. Ads may still be requested using restricted delivery where the provider and your privacy choices permit. An ad request is not a guarantee an ad will be available.

A saved Apple refusal is not followed by another automatic request to permit tracking. Required regional privacy options remain accessible explicitly through Settings → Ad privacy; they cannot override Apple's refusal. You can change the age range and regional choices there, or change Apple's tracking permission in the iPhone or iPad Settings app. Changes stop new ad requests while they are applied. When an advertising SDK is already initialized, the app requests a restart to apply the new setup fully.

Optional Facebook and Google measurement

Meta App Events and Google Analytics for Firebase are enabled only for eligible adults after completed consent, Apple's Allowed tracking permission and the reviewed regional checks. Unsupported or unknown regional measurement states remain disabled. Google Analytics also requires the verified Production app environment and does not initialize for Sandbox or TestFlight. A Google Ads account link is separate from Analytics and is not implied by this notice.

When permitted, these SDKs can process app activation or session information, app and installation identifiers, app version, connection information and SDK diagnostics for measurement. App-owned activation and verified-purchase reporting are controlled by the native privacy gates. Meta automatic app/purchase logging and advertiser-ID collection stay disabled. Firebase collection and consent categories start denied; advertising personalization and IDFV collection stay disabled. Firebase may emit its own session, first-open or automatically observed purchase information once authorized; that observation does not prove the game delivered an item.

A separate verified-purchase conversion is eligible only for a new matching Production transaction that was verified and durably delivered. It includes product, amount, currency, time and a hashed event identifier for duplicate prevention. Historical, restored, revoked, undelivered and Sandbox transactions are excluded. We do not attach your raw Game Center identifier, game credential, Apple transaction signature, email address or phone number to these app-owned conversion events.

Withdrawing eligibility stops new app-owned measurement. Restart the app to apply an initialized SDK's setup fully. Changing a choice does not erase data already sent or independently held by a provider; use the provider's controls or contact us for help.

Optional play reminders and ratings

Play reminders are off by default. After a completed puzzle the game may invite you to enable them; Apple asks permission only after you choose Enable. One nonrepeating reminder may be scheduled for 18:00 after at least 24 hours away. Returning to the foreground or turning reminders off cancels Witchdoku's pending reminders. Settings → Play reminders provides opt-out and a link to the app's notification settings. Delivery is local to your device; we do not operate a remote notification marketing service for this feature.

The game may request an App Store review after several distinct completed puzzles. Apple controls whether its review interface appears. There is no reward or gameplay requirement for rating the game, and we do not receive the rating you choose through this interface.

Deletion, retention and backups

Settings → Delete My Data requires confirmation. It requests deletion of game profile, progress, ranking eligibility, settings and inventory, and this device's account data and signed iCloud payload. Interrupted requests stay pending and can be retried; pending does not mean deletion completed. A deletion marker prevents an old copy from restoring erased progress. Disconnected devices must reconnect; their local copies cannot be erased remotely immediately.

Live game data is retained while needed to provide the account and until an applicable deletion request is completed. Limited transaction, reward, anti-replay, security and deletion records can remain to prevent duplicate grants, support eligible purchase restoration and meet applicable obligations. A retry acknowledgement lasts 30 days; short-lived authentication proof hashes are valid for six minutes. Expired authentication records are removed on subsequent authentication changes, so inactive records can remain longer.

Hosting and recovery backups can retain older copies for their retention window; in-app deletion does not instantly erase backups. Restoration must preserve deletion markers and transaction integrity. Contact us about copies we hold or a pending deletion. Provider-held records are governed separately by their policies. Deleting game data does not refund or cancel an Apple purchase.

These providers may process connection and service information outside your country. Their policies and controls are linked below.

Depending on your country, rights may include access, correction, deletion, objection and withdrawal of consent. Withdrawal applies to future processing and does not undo earlier lawful processing. We use HTTPS and access controls; no service can promise absolute security. Contact us for requests or help directing a request to a provider. Please do not send passwords, payment details or advertising identifiers.
