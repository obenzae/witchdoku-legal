Privacy for the Witchdoku iOS events beta

This notice covers internal TestFlight successors of Witchdoku 1.0 build 6 that link here. It does not announce an App Store release or public availability. Eligible adults can receive live advertising after the privacy steps below. Child, teen and unknown-age advertising is disabled. Earlier development builds remain covered by their linked notice and the existing Witchdoku privacy policy.

Honesty Games operates Witchdoku. Contact benlalououssama@gmail.com about privacy, access, correction or deletion, including a request concerning a child. Please do not email passwords, payment details or advertising identifiers.

Information used by this build

Online play requires an internet connection and Game Center sign-in. Apple-signed app information selects the separate TestFlight service; its accounts and inventory are distinct from the earlier development service and the public-release service. We verify an Apple-signed, app-scoped player identifier and associate it with an internal game account. We do not receive your Apple password or payment-card information.

Device and iCloud storage

The device holds account-bound save and retry journals, presentation preferences and an age-range choice. We ask for an age range, not a birth date. When iCloud is available, a signed game snapshot is backed up to your private CloudKit database. A client-written backup cannot grant inventory or replace the server's authoritative progress. Apple controls Game Center and iCloud accounts.

Advertising and rewarded videos

This build uses Unity LevelPlay to mediate ironSource Ads, Unity Ads, Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network. The network that serves a particular ad depends on eligibility, privacy choices and available inventory. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) supplies applicable consent and privacy-choice forms. Meta Audience Network supplies in-game ads. This successor also enables optional Meta App Events for eligible, consenting adults under the additional conditions below. Automatic event logging and advertiser-ID collection remain disabled, as do the legacy Firebase startup paths. Earlier builds, including build 6, keep their separate Meta App Events integration off.

Advertising providers may process device and advertising identifiers allowed by platform settings, IP addresses and approximate location inferred from connection information, device/app information, ad requests, impressions, views, interactions, performance information, crash reports and diagnostics. These support advertising, measurement, frequency limits, service improvement, security and fraud prevention. Google and Meta SDK privacy declarations include advertising, device-identifier and diagnostic data; Google also declares approximate-location, performance and product-interaction data. A request for contextual or restricted ads does not mean that no information is processed.

The app supplies LevelPlay with a pseudonymous ad-user identifier obtained from the game authority. It does not supply a raw Game Center identifier or game authentication token. This identifier is pseudonymous, not anonymous. For rewarded videos, LevelPlay sends the authority a signed completion event with that identifier, an event ID, reward amount and verification information. One valid event grants one Continue once. Closing an ad or clicking an advertiser's install button is not a reward condition.

When ads are available, a banner can appear at the bottom of screens. An interstitial opportunity occurs after every two accepted campaign wins. Game-over rewarded videos are optional, and Restart remains free. Unavailable ads or pending reward verification do not remove free Restart.

Optional Meta app measurement

Meta App Events helps us measure app activation and the results of Facebook and Instagram promotion. It is separate from in-game Meta Audience Network ads. The app starts this measurement only after the adult age choice, completed UMP privacy flow, Apple tracking permission set to Allowed, and the existing regional consent checks. In the EEA, the required consent purposes and explicit Meta additional consent must be present. Unknown or unsupported regional privacy states, including the currently unreviewed US/GPP measurement path, keep this measurement disabled. Refusing measurement does not prevent playing.

When those checks permit it, the app manually sends app activation information to Meta. The SDK may process app/device and installation identifiers, app version, session timing, IP address and connection/device information for measurement and fraud prevention. Automatic app/purchase logging and SDK advertising-ID collection are disabled. We do not attach a raw Game Center identifier, game authentication token, Apple transaction signature or raw Apple transaction ID to these events.

The manual purchase reporter accepts only a new Apple-verified Production transaction made after the permitted measurement session began and durably delivered by the matching Production game service. Its event contains the product, amount, currency, purchase time and an event identifier derived from a hash. Historical, restored, revoked, undelivered and Sandbox transactions are excluded. Internal TestFlight purchases therefore do not generate these purchase-conversion events and do not establish revenue.

Use Settings → Ad privacy or Apple tracking settings to change your choices. Withdrawing eligibility stops new app-owned measurement events. If a provider SDK has already initialized, close and reopen the app to apply the new setup fully. Already queued or transmitted provider data and provider-held records are subject to that provider’s processing and retention; changing a choice does not erase earlier records.

Google Ads conversion measurement preparation

This successor includes Google Analytics for Firebase Core 12.17.0 for planned Google and YouTube campaign measurement. Its separate measurement gates remain off until the Firebase/Analytics project and Google Ads link are reviewed. Installing the SDK is not evidence that conversions are being collected or attributed. The Analytics Core package excludes its IDFA and on-device conversion modules; app-instance and installation identifiers can still be used when collection is enabled.

The prepared measurement path requires a verified Production app environment, an adult age choice, Apple tracking permission set to Allowed, completed consent, and the reviewed regional consent checks. It does not initialize Google Analytics for Sandbox or TestFlight. Collection and all four consent-mode categories default to denied; ad personalization stays denied even on an eligible path. No email address, phone number, raw Game Center identifier or game authentication proof is attached.

On an eligible future Production path, the SDK can collect first-open and session events and automatically observed in-app purchases. Automatically observed purchases do not establish that the game delivered an item. A separate r31_verified_purchase conversion is emitted only after a new matching Production transaction is verified and durably delivered by the game authority. It excludes restored, historical, revoked, undelivered and Sandbox transactions and uses a hashed event identifier for duplicate prevention. Google Ads must import the intended verified conversion after its account link is reviewed. No campaign attribution or real-money sale is certified by this notice.

Age, consent and privacy choices

Witchdoku is intended for children and adults. In this internal beta, only the adult range can initialize advertising. Under-13, teen and unknown-age choices do not start the ad SDK. The planned child path uses child-directed, restricted-consent and do-not-sell signals, but it remains disabled pending provider and device review. Online gameplay still uses the account and game information described above. Parents can contact us about a child's information and review Apple's Game Center and iCloud controls.

On the eligible adult path, the app refreshes UMP consent information and presents any required form before requesting ads. A failed consent request does not silently authorize advertising. Apple's tracking permission is separate and is requested only on the eligible adult path before Home is revealed. Declining it does not prevent playing; advertising may use the restricted path permitted by the provider and your choices. The app does not force tracking permission or require a registered advertising identifier to play.

Use Settings → Ad privacy to revisit the age range, retry consent or open required provider privacy options. Changes pause new ad requests and presentation. If the ad SDK has already started, the app asks you to close and reopen Witchdoku to apply the new setup fully. You can also change Apple's tracking permission in iOS Settings. The integration sends LevelPlay a do-not-sell signal and passes the applicable age restrictions to its mediated networks. Google publisher first-party identifiers and publisher personalization are disabled by this build. Meta Audience Network receives Limited Data Use settings, with Meta determining the applicable region from connection information. Its mixed-audience setting does not enable ads for minors. Audience Network reads Apple's actual tracking-permission status on supported iOS versions; we do not substitute a tracking authorization. These controls do not make advertising anonymous or remove every provider record.

TestFlight in-app purchases use Apple's sandbox and do not charge money. Sandbox purchases are not evidence of real-money sales.

Deletion and purchase restoration

Settings → Delete My Data requires explicit confirmation. It requests removal of the server profile, progress, ranking eligibility, settings, feedback and inventory and removal of this device's account data and signed iCloud payload. A small deletion marker prevents an older backup from restoring erased progress. Interrupted requests remain pending and can be retried; a pending request is not completed deletion. The complete signed TestFlight device-and-cloud deletion flow remains under verification. Contact us for assistance; this notice does not certify that flow or promise immediate erasure. Disconnected devices must reconnect, and their local copies cannot be remotely erased immediately.

Deletion does not delete your Apple account or refund or cancel Apple purchases. Starting again requires an explicit choice. Eligible permanent cosmetics require fresh Apple verification to restore; spent or deleted consumables are not regranted. Limited pseudonymous purchase, reward-event and account-binding records remain to prevent duplicate grants and to support eligible restoration.

Retention and recovery copies

Live game information is retained while the account is active, until deletion or the end of this internal beta service. Limited transaction, anti-replay and deletion records remain for security, purchase integrity and eligible restoration. A deletion acknowledgement is usable for retries for 30 days; short-lived proof hashes have six-minute validity. Expired authentication records are pruned when authentication state next changes, so inactive records can remain longer.

The TestFlight authority currently has Fly volume backups configured for five-day retention. This is a retention setting, not a verified guarantee of deletion or of what a restored backup could contain. In-app deletion does not immediately erase provider backups. Earlier development recovery copies remain separate and may require operator review. Contact us to request review and deletion of copies we hold. Backup deletion and restoration propagation and the full signed device-and-cloud deletion flow remain internal QA requirements before public rollout.
