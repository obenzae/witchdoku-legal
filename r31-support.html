Help us understand the problem

Include your device model, iOS version, Witchdoku version and what happened. If useful, attach a screenshot with personal information removed. Never send passwords, payment card details or sign-in codes.

Sign-in and progress

Witchdoku requires an internet connection and Game Center sign-in. Check that you are signed in to the Game Center account you used to play. Contact support before deleting or reinstalling the app if you are having trouble accessing saved progress.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases to recover eligible permanent character purchases associated with your Apple account. Consumable tools are credited after purchase verification. If a purchase is pending or missing, contact support; avoid buying the same item again to resolve an error.

Privacy and deletion

The app provides privacy controls and Settings → Delete My Data. You can also contact the support address above about your game data. Deleting game data does not cancel purchases or issue an Apple refund.
