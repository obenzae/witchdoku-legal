Witchdoku macOS privacy notice

Updated November 26, 2026

This notice describes the native macOS version of Witchdoku that links here. It uses Game Center, an online game service, StoreKit and private iCloud storage to provide account-bound progress and purchases.

No advertising or cross-company tracking

The native Mac app does not show advertisements or use advertising, Google consent, Meta App Events or Firebase Analytics SDKs. It does not access an advertising identifier or track your activity across other companies' apps or websites. It does not request Apple's tracking permission or present a Google GDPR advertising consent form. This behavior applies in every region. Settings → Privacy on Mac explains the data needed to operate the game.

Game accounts and progress

Online play requires an internet connection and Game Center sign-in. Apple supplies a signed, app-scoped player identifier. Our game service verifies it and associates it with an internal game account. We do not receive your Apple password or payment-card information. App Store, TestFlight and earlier development environments keep separate service accounts and inventory.

The game service stores campaign and tutorial progress, puzzle sessions, scores, tool balances, character ownership and selection, achievements or daily rewards, streak dates, settings, and account or transaction records needed to operate these features. Rankings use accepted game results. Connection-derived country information may accompany ranking entries; it is not a statement of nationality. The service and its hosting provider process connection information to deliver requests and protect account and purchase integrity.

Local and iCloud storage

The Mac stores account-bound pending-operation and completion journals and presentation preferences in its app storage. Authentication credentials use protected Keychain storage. When available, the game can save a signed snapshot to your private CloudKit database. The online game service remains authoritative; an iCloud copy cannot grant purchases or replace validated progress. Apple controls Game Center and iCloud account settings.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app purchases and provides product prices and transaction information. Our service verifies matching app, product, account and environment information before delivering an item. Purchase and revocation records prevent duplicate grants and support eligible restoration. Consumables already spent are not replenished by Restore. Apple manages billing, cancellations and refund decisions. The Mac app does not report purchases to advertising or analytics providers.

Deletion and retention

Settings → Delete My Data requires confirmation. It requests deletion of game profile, progress, ranking eligibility, settings and inventory, and this device's account data and signed iCloud payload. Interrupted requests stay pending and can be retried; pending does not mean deletion completed. A deletion marker prevents an old copy from restoring erased progress. Disconnected devices must reconnect; their local copies cannot be erased remotely immediately.

Live game data is retained while needed to provide the account and until an applicable deletion request is completed. Limited transaction, anti-replay, security and deletion records can remain to prevent duplicate grants, support eligible purchase restoration and meet applicable obligations. A retry acknowledgement lasts 30 days; short-lived authentication proof hashes are valid for six minutes. Expired authentication records are removed on subsequent authentication changes, so inactive records can remain longer.

Hosting and recovery backups can retain older copies for their retention window; in-app deletion does not instantly erase backups. Restoration must preserve deletion markers and transaction integrity. Contact us about copies we hold or a pending deletion. Provider-held records are governed separately by their policies. Deleting game data does not refund or cancel an Apple purchase.

Providers and your rights

We use Apple for Game Center, StoreKit and private CloudKit; Fly.io for the online game authority and storage; and GitHub Pages for this notice. These providers may process connection and service information outside your country.

Depending on your country, rights may include access, correction, deletion and objection. We use HTTPS and access controls; no service can promise absolute security. Contact us for requests or help directing a request to a provider. Please do not send passwords, payment details or authentication credentials.
